At depths where sunlight cannot penetrate, marine organisms have
 evolved their own intricate
  language of light. Firefly 
  squid flash brilliant turquoise signals to communicate across the abyss, while anglerfish suspend glowing 
  lures to entice unwary prey into razor-sharp jaws. These cold, chemical reactions—produced through the inter
  action of luciferin and luciferase—convert nearly 100% of their energy into light without losing a fraction to heat, 
  putting human electrical efficiency to shame.
